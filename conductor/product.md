# Product Guide: WeatherGenerator

## 1. Vision & Purpose
WeatherGenerator is a machine learning-based Earth system model. Its primary purpose is to provide robust, ML-driven dynamics for earth system modeling and to facilitate data downscaling.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Climate Researchers**: Academics and scientists studying climate change and earth systems.
- **Machine Learning Scientists and Nerds**: ML practitioners experimenting with advanced neural network architectures, large-scale training, and geospatial data.

## 3. Core Goals
- **ML Earth System Model**: Develop a robust, versatile model capable of simulating earth system dynamics accurately.
- **Data Downscaling**: Utilize the model to enhance the resolution of existing reanalysis and forecast data, providing high-fidelity datasets.

## 4. Key Features
- **Multi-dataset Training**: Capable of ingesting and learning from a wide range of datasets, including historical reanalyses, forecast data, and raw observations.
- **High-Performance ML (PyTorch)**: Leverages modern GPU hardware and optimizations like flash-attention for efficient, large-scale distributed training using PyTorch.
